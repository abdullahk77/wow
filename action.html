Phase 03

Action.
Why you don’t follow through, even when you intend to.

You don’t avoid action because you don’t care.

You hesitate.
Right before you begin.

That small moment
is where things shift.

You’ve been here.

You know what needs to be done.

You think about starting.
You get ready.

Then you pause.
Just for a moment.

And something else takes your attention.

You delay.
You adjust.
You wait.

And the moment passes.

Not because you decided to stop.

Because you didn’t begin.

Starting is not neutral.

It feels heavier than avoiding.

There is uncertainty.

There is effort.

There is resistance.

So your attention moves somewhere easier.

Avoiding feels lighter.

So you move toward it.

Not as a decision.

As a reaction.

Even when you begin,

the same thing happens again.

If something feels difficult,

you slow down.

You switch.

Or you stop.

Because continuing carries weight too.

Once you see this clearly,

you begin to notice that moment before you start.

And how easily it shifts.

You don’t need more discipline.

You need a lighter way to begin.

When starting feels lighter,
you begin more often.

Make things smaller.

Start before it feels perfect.

Remove what slows you down.

Now the moment of starting feels different.

And once you begin,
it becomes easier to continue than to stop.

What you do now feeds back into how you see yourself.

And you begin to notice it more clearly.
