Phase 01

Identity.
Why you keep returning to the same place.

You don’t start from zero.

You start from what already feels like you.

Most of the time,
you don’t even notice it.

You’ve seen this happen.

You decide to change something.

You begin with intention.

For a while, it feels different.

Then something pulls you back.

And you return to what you were doing before.

It feels like a lack of discipline.

But it isn’t.

What you do repeatedly
starts to feel normal.

And what feels normal
starts to feel like you.

From there,
you don’t question it.

You continue it.

If you often delay things,
you begin to expect delay.

If you lose focus easily,
you stop expecting consistency.

If you don’t follow through,
you stop trusting yourself to finish.

Not consciously.

Just gradually.

So the next time you try to change something,

you’re not starting fresh.

You’re starting from what already feels true.

And that makes things heavier than they should be.

This is why effort doesn’t last.

You can push for a while.

But eventually,
you return to what feels familiar.

Not because you don’t care.

Because it feels like you.

Once you see this clearly,

you stop assuming something is wrong with you.

And start noticing what has been repeating.

You are not stuck.

You are continuing what has been reinforced.

And what has been reinforced
can be seen more clearly.

But there’s something else involved.

Even when you see this,
your attention still moves.

And where it goes
shapes what happens next.
