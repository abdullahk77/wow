Method

The Action Approach

Most people don’t have an action problem.

They have a hesitation problem.

They think.
They plan.
They prepare.

But they don’t move.

It usually looks like this

  • You wait to feel ready
  • You try to understand everything first
  • You delay starting
  • You tell yourself you’ll begin “properly” later

Clarity becomes a requirement.

So action never begins.

What’s actually happening

Action creates clarity.

But most people reverse it.

They wait for clarity before acting.

And that keeps them stuck.

You don’t need to be ready.

You need to start before you are.

The shift

Instead of thinking your way forward…

You act your way forward.

Not perfectly.
Not completely.
But immediately.

What this changes

  • Resistance drops
  • Momentum builds
  • Clarity follows

And once you begin,

the next step becomes visible.

You don’t need a full plan.

You need a first move.

But here’s the real problem

Even when you act,

you return to the same patterns.

Because action follows identity.

If your identity doesn’t change,
your actions won’t hold.
